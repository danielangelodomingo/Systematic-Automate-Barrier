Systematic Automate Barrier (SAB)


What it is?

The Systematic Automate Barrier (SAB) is a smart, high-tech environmental shield system designed to protect communities from extreme weather and climate change.

What it does?

It catches extreme weather and turns it into useful resources through three main features:

1.) ​Blocks bad weather: Acts as an adaptive shield to protect vulnerable areas from disasters.
2.) ​Makes clean electricity: Harnesses heavy rain energy and extreme heat to generate green power.
3.) ​Creates clean water: Collects heavy rain and purifies atmospheric moisture into drinkable water.

How Does the Program Work?

° You type the city, province/state, and country (for example: Florida, Philippines or Tokyo, Tokyo, Japan). The system locates the exact place on the global map without getting confused by places with similar names.
° It fetches live weather data (temperature, rain level, and UV index) directly from weather satellites over the internet.
° You do not need to measure the land size manually. The system measures the city's surface area automatically in square meters.
° Shows Color-Coded Threat Alerts:
​🟢 Green: Safe / Normal weather
​🟡 Yellow: Moderate risk weather
​🟠 Orange: High risk weather
​🔴 Red: Severe / Dangerous threat
° It calculates the monetary value of generated power using live currency exchange rates for the target country USD, JPY, EUR, etc.
° If you make a typo when answering (like typing "yws" instead of "yes"), it automatically understands or asks you again without shutting down.
